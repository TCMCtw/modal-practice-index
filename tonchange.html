同主音調式變化PARALLEL MODES

C 起音 · 逐次降音

這份教材說明「同主音調式變化」怎麼練;每行譜例旁都有播放鈕,點了就唱給你聽。請邊看邊聽。These notes show how the parallel-mode drill works. Every staff line has a play button next to it; tap to hear that line sung. Listen as you read.

一、這個練習做什麼

整條練習裡,只有主音 C 不動;其他的音,每一步只改一個,音階就換成下一個調式。和降七、升四兩個練習相比:它們在過程中,起音會移動半音;這個練習從頭到尾都在同一個 C 上,主音完全不動。

Only the tonic C stays fixed; at each step, exactly one other note changes, and the scale becomes the next mode. The Lower-7 and Raise-4 drills move their starting note by a semitone partway through; this drill stays on the same C from beginning to end.

二、方向:逐次降音

練習以逐次降音一個方向進行:從 Lydian 出發,每一步降一個音,走到 Locrian。反向只是把各段順序倒過來(段內的音階仍然向上唱),和降七、升四一樣,一個方向就夠。

The drill works in one direction: from Lydian, one note lowered per step, down to Locrian. The reverse is just the same chain backwards (each scale still sung upward), so one direction suffices, as with Lower-7 and Raise-4.

黑色粗體是調式名稱;金色是變化音,青色是它變成的自然音,和下面譜例的唱名顏色一致。

Mode names are in bold; the altered note is gold and the natural note it becomes is teal, the same colors as the solfege in the notation below.

三、每個調式怎麼唱

每個調式唱兩列。第一列是「同主音 do 列」:整首練習主音 C 固定唱 do;走到這個調式時累積改了哪幾個音,那幾個音就唱變化唱名(fi、te、me、le、ra、se),其他音的唱名不動。第二列是「首調列」:同一條音高不動,改用這個調式自己的母大調首調,從頭重唱一遍。

Each mode has two lines. The first is the fixed-do line: the tonic C is sung do for the whole drill, and whichever notes have been altered up to this mode take their altered syllables (fi, te, me, le, ra, se) while the rest stay put. The second is the solfege line: the same pitches re-sung from the top in this mode's own parent-major syllables.

大調這一站,兩列唱名完全相同(都是 do re mi fa sol la ti do),只唱一遍,所以每個方向是 13 列,不是 14 列。

At the major-scale stop the two lines would be identical (do re mi fa sol la ti do), so it is sung only once; that is why each direction has 13 lines, not 14.

這個練習新增兩個唱名:le(A♭)與 se(G♭);和 te、me、ra 一樣,只出現在同主音 do 列。

This drill adds two syllables, le (A♭) and se (G♭); like te, me and ra, they appear only in the fixed-do lines.

四、聽什麼

每次只有一個音變,先找出是哪個音。同主音 do 列裡,C 永遠唱 do,變的是其他音;首調列裡,同一個 C 的角色一直在換:依序被唱成 fa、do、sol、re、la、mi、ti。起音的實際音高始終相同,角色一直換,這是練習的核心。

Only one note changes at a time; find that note first. In the fixed-do lines C is always do and the other notes move; in the solfege lines the same C keeps changing roles: fa, do, sol, re, la, mi, ti in turn. The starting pitch never moves; its role keeps changing. That is the heart of this drill.

五、譜面與音檔對照

每列 8 個二分音符(4 小節),上聲部 C4 起、下聲部低八度,全曲 13 列。每個調式兩列,先同主音 do 列、再首調列;大調只一列。每列起點上方標明調式與唱法,例如「Mixolydian · 同主音 do(降二個 ti→te)」、「Mixolydian · 首調(sol 起)」。變化音帶降記號。

Each line is 8 half notes (4 bars); the upper staff starts on C4 and the lower staff an octave below; 13 lines in all. Each mode takes two lines, the fixed-do line first, then the solfege line; the major scale takes one. A label above each line names the mode and the singing plan, e.g. "Mixolydian · fixed do (2nd lowering, ti→te)" or "Mixolydian · solfege (starts on sol)". Altered notes carry flats.
